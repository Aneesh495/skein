03 / SYMBOLIC STRUCTURE

One event, a shared decision graph.

Dashed edges select false; solid edges select true. Identical subfunctions share a node. Skipped noise variables integrate to one.

Minimal sufficient noise assignments

The event is monotone in its noise variables.

prime implicant
disk_a_noise=1 & disk_b_noise=1
power_noise=1

Implicants can overlap. Their probabilities must not be added.

Exact numerator polynomial

1*disk_a_noise*disk_b_noise + 1*power_noise + -1*power_noise*disk_a_noise*disk_b_noise

The coefficients are exact. The monomials describe prior probabilities, not independent failure scenarios.
